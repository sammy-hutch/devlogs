## New Font
**Project:**

[n00b-studios](https://github.com/sammy-hutch/n00b-studios)


**Details:** 

New font: StarScript. Sci-fi stencil-style font. currently ascender of 8, descender of 3, typical width of 4 for most characters, 5 where necessary.

The aim was to make a font that looked futuristic, blocky & sleek, at as small a resolution as possible.

Some kerning for particular letter combos still needed, and some issues with spacing on certain characters, same as with previous font.
